# Open DB of Coffee grind distributions

This is a repo for sharing data generated by https://github.com/jgagneastro 's coffeegrindsize application.

## Download the Application

**Easy Setup - No Installation Required!**

Download the Coffee Grind Distribution Analyzer for your operating system:

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/julowe/coffee-grind-distribution/releases)
2. Download the latest version for your platform:
   - **Windows**: `CoffeeGrindAnalyzer-Windows.zip`
   - **macOS**: `CoffeeGrindAnalyzer-macOS.zip`
   - **Linux**: `CoffeeGrindAnalyzer-Linux.zip`
3. Extract the ZIP file
4. Run the application:
   - **Windows**: Double-click `CoffeeGrindAnalyzer.exe`
   - **macOS**: Double-click `CoffeeGrindAnalyzer` (you may need to right-click and select "Open" the first time)
   - **Linux**: Make executable with `chmod +x CoffeeGrindAnalyzer` then run `./CoffeeGrindAnalyzer`

The application will help you analyze your coffee grind photos and prepare data for submission to this repository.

# Background

Coffee particle distribution is important to get a good pourover, but depending on the grinder and the bean, the distributions may vary significantly. Up until now, there has been no public effort to collaborate on gathering this data - a problem this project aims to solve.

# Prerequisites to submit your data

You will need:

* a coffee grinder
* a few (ideally speciality grade) coffee beans
* a camera (8+ megapixels)
* The Coffee Grind Distribution Analyzer application (see "Download the Application" section above)
* a bit of your time and effort

## Quick Start

1. **Download the application** from the [Releases page](https://github.com/julowe/coffee-grind-distribution/releases)
2. **Extract and run** the application for your operating system
3. **Follow the in-app instructions** to analyze your coffee grind photo
4. **Submit your data** by creating a pull request with the generated files

# Instructions

## Step 1: Prepare Your Sample

* Get a sheet of white A4 (210 x 297 mm) or standard US letter sized paper (8.5 x 11 in). If using a manual or single dosing electric grinder, put in a single bean and grind at a pourover medium-fine setting onto the paper, moving the paper or grinder to distribute the grinds evenly across the paper. If using a non single-dosing machine, do your best to output as little coffee onto the paper as possible. If there's any grinds stuck on your grinder, gently brush them onto the paper as well. Gently use a fork or chopstick to move around any clusters of grinds stuck together. Your paper should look something like: https://i.imgur.com/A1C2RPA.jpg

## Step 2: Take a Photo

* Take a photo with a camera or phone with at least 8 megapixels, with good lighting, no shadows, as perpendicular to the paper as possible, and showing as much of the entire paper on frame as possible. Do this carefully as your submission cannot be accepted if it does not meet these requirements.

## Step 3: Use the Application

* Open the Coffee Grind Distribution Analyzer application
* Click **"Open Image"** and select your coffee grind photo
* Enter your **Grinder Name** and **Username**
* Click **"Analyze & Save Data"**
* The application will create a folder with all necessary files for submission

## Step 4: Submit Your Data

* Upload your raw image to imgur.com for validation
* Fork this repository
* Copy the generated folder from your home directory (~/CoffeeGrindData/[timestamp]_[username]/) to the `data/` directory in your fork
* Update the README.md file in your folder with the imgur link and any additional details
* Create a pull request with your data

* Enjoy a cup of coffee after your hard work!
